# Customer discovery workshop (30 minutes)

## Role-play brief

You are the FDE. Your interviewer plays the VP of Operations at a company that wants “an AI agent to automate support.” You have not yet seen their systems or error rates.

## Opening questions

1. Which **single workflow** creates the most cost or risk today?
2. Who performs it? What triggers it? What systems does it touch?
3. What is baseline handling time, throughput, rework, and error severity?
4. Which decisions require a human? Who can authorize actions?
5. Where is the source of truth? How frequently do policies and permissions change?
6. What data is sensitive or contractually restricted?
7. What integrations and production owners exist?
8. What is the smallest useful pilot? Who signs off on success?
9. What deadline and budget are fixed? What scope is flexible?
10. How will we measure adoption, not just demo quality?

## Interview deliverable: one-page scope statement

- **User and job-to-be-done:** ...
- **Current baseline:** ...
- **MVP flow / explicit exclusions:** ...
- **Sources of truth + data ownership:** ...
- **Success metrics, sample sizes, and owners:** ...
- **Failure severity and review policy:** ...
- **Dependencies, blockers, and rollback:** ...
- **Week 1 / week 3 / week 6 deliverables:** ...

## Example negotiated MVP

“Assist human agents with policy-grounded draft responses for one high-volume ticket category. No autonomous refunds. Evaluate against 200 consented/de-identified historical cases, pilot with 10 agents, measure handling time and correction rate against a baseline, and keep manual workflow as fallback.”

The numbers are illustrative exercise assumptions, not empirical customer benchmarks.
